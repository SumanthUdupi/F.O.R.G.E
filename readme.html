THE OFFICE IS THE APP

There is no menu. You click the building.

Click a person → talk

Their record, your conversation. Ask is enforced read-only. Do runs Claude Code live in your workspace and streams into the thread.

Walk into a room → work

Ideas and GitHub repos go to the Lab, plans compose in the Directorate, spending lives in the Treasury, approvals wait at your desk.

It learns → you sign

Outcomes land in a ledger; reliability is derived; routing shifts. Five failures and an agent loses the next stage. Nothing changes without your approval.

NO CEO

Six accountable seats. Each refuses something, in writing.

The Chair

Convenes and records. REFUSES to break a tie — deadlocks come to you.

Chief of Works

Correct and verified. REFUSES "done" without evidence.

Chief of Ledger

Cost against value. REFUSES to trade correctness for tokens.

Chief of Workforce

Trust, measured. REFUSES to retire an agent before extracting what it knows.

Chief of Intent

Your words, both ways. REFUSES to guess at a missing requirement.

Chief of Memory

What it knows, graded. REFUSES to answer from stale memory.
